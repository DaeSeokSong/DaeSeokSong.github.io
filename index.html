01 / CURRENT RESEARCH

RevolverNet

A testbed for learning, routing, removing, and replacing functional subnetworks.

A bullet is a detachable subnetwork trained to own one or more classes or functions. A chamber is the slot that receives it. A trigger routes each input to the responsible bullet.

The metaphor is practical: I want to rotate through specialized modules during learning and inference, then remove or replace one and measure exactly which behavior changes. The long-term goal is not only a smaller model, but an AI system whose functional parts people can inspect and control.

99.4–99.8% of the performance lost under naive swaps was recovered after one-time alignment.

Result from two completed image-classification conditions and 24 directed module transfers. Preliminary, unpublished independent work.

Four models per condition shared the dataset, architecture, and role assignment but used different initializations. A 512-image validation set estimated one orthogonal Procrustes transform; transferred bullets were not retrained.

Test whether role ownership and transfer survive unseen data, unseen models, and new tasks. I also plan to study when a new chamber and bullet should be added, without treating that condition as a completed result.
